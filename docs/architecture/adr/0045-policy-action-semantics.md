# ADR-0045: What each policy action does in step 5.2 (enforced, requested or only recorded)

**Status:** Accepted; implemented in step 5.2. **The `QUARANTINE`, `WARN` and `URL_REWRITE` rows (and the alternatives "writing quarantine rows in 5.2" and "URL_REWRITE by asking the URL engine to rewrite after the decision") are superseded by [ADR-0049](0049-policy-copy-body-edits-banners-and-url-rewrite.md)** (step 5.3, accepted and implemented); the other rows stay in force. Step 5.2 — verified locally on 2026-11-11, [§13](../../plan/steps/16-policy-engine.md#13-verification-results); implementation notes in [§12](../../plan/steps/16-policy-engine.md#12-deviations-log) (ARCHIVE and ALERT are requested and verified up to the dormant `email.archive` buffer and the mail-sink; WARN body banners are recorded only; the archive consumer is step 8.1, the quarantine store step 5.3). Design: [step 5.2](../../plan/steps/16-policy-engine.md) P9, P19–P21. Works with [ADR-0043](0043-policy-dsl-and-deterministic-evaluator.md) and [ADR-0044](0044-policy-evaluation-placement-and-delivery-gating.md). Implements the 5.2 side of [ADR-0037](0037-dlp-policy-actions-and-lifecycle-binding.md) decision 6 (DLP binding) and uses [ADR-0030](0030-url-rewriting-and-click-tokens.md) (rewritten copies) unchanged.

**Context.** The brief lists nine actions and requires that an action is never claimed as enforced when it is only recorded. Several downstream stages do not exist yet: the quarantine store, release workflow and digests (5.3), archiving and journaling (8.1), full notifications (6.2). Inbound mail has already been accepted at SMTP time when policy runs. Body banners would require MIME body rewriting, which only the URL engine's rewriter does today, at scan time.

**Decision.** Every stored action carries a status: `enforced` (5.2 performs it), `requested` (5.2 hands it to a stage through a durable outbox event; that stage performs it), `recorded` (stored and visible, nothing performed), `not_applicable` (the final disposition makes it moot, e.g. TAG on a quarantined message).

If the round was not rewritten: `ifNotRewritten = deliver` (default) records the skip reason; `ifNotRewritten = quarantine` raises the disposition to QUARANTINE. Enabling rewriting is a 4.3 tenant setting. | enforced when a rewritten copy exists or the hold applies; recorded otherwise                       |

The evaluation row is the durable record 8.1 can reconcile from (the dormant queue is a bounded lossy buffer).                                                                                                                                                                             | requested (performed by 8.1)                                                                        |
| `ALERT`            | non-terminal | `mail.policy.notification_requested.v1` (`kind = policy_alert`) to `email.notifications`; the notification-worker e-mails the organization's active users holding `policy:publish` through the transactional relay (postfix-notify). Ids, codes and names of policy objects only; never message content.                                                                                                             | requested (sent by the notification-worker, at least once)                                          |

TAG, WARN and URL_REWRITE are `not_applicable` when the final disposition is not ALLOW; a later release delivers the object the release names (5.3 may re-apply edits). ARCHIVE and ALERT apply to every disposition.

**Alternatives considered.** Inbound REJECT as a DSN to the envelope sender: rejected (backscatter to forged senders). Rejecting inbound mail at SMTP time: not possible, policy runs post-queue after scanning (ADR-0022, ADR-0026). WARN body banners through a new MIME body rewriter: deferred, it duplicates the 4.3 rewriter's MIME-safety work and breaks signatures for little gain. URL_REWRITE by asking the URL engine to rewrite after the decision: rejected for 5.2 (a second scan pass, token key custody in another flow, latency). Writing quarantine rows in 5.2: rejected, the store's schema and release semantics are 5.3's.

**Consequences.** Recipients see `X-EG-Policy-*` headers only in policy copies; headers of that name already present in an original are not stripped when no copy is made (documented limitation). Inbound DKIM signatures covering a prefixed Subject no longer verify downstream (as with URL rewriting; the tenant's mailbox platform must trust the gateway). Alerts and notices are at-least-once.
