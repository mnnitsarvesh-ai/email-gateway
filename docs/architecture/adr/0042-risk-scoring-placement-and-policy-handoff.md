# ADR-0042: Risk scoring at round completion in the email-worker; per-round score rows; hand-off to policy 5.2 and quarantine 5.3

**Status:** Accepted; implemented in step 5.1 — verified locally on 2026-11-11, [§13](../../plan/steps/15-risk-scoring.md#13-verification-results). Implementation notes ([§12](../../plan/steps/15-risk-scoring.md#12-deviations-log)): the round's child rows are read `FOR SHARE` on both the completion and the sandbox path; metrics are recorded after the commit; a round whose message row is gone gets no row (`scan_risk_skipped_total`, subtracted by `RiskScoreMissing`); a draft or missing snapshot version falls back as `missing`; the pass-through hold is verified through the stack (GTUBE held as `risk_recommendation`), and decision 7 is written into [contracts §27](../contracts.md#27-risk-scoring-step-51). Design: [step 5.1](../../plan/steps/15-risk-scoring.md) R13–R17, §3, §4. Works with [ADR-0041](0041-risk-scoring-model.md). Amends [ADR-0026](0026-security-scan-pipeline.md) (the completion transaction also writes the round's risk score; the round snapshots the tenant's active scoring profile when it opens) and [ADR-0040](0040-sandbox-engine-dispatch-results-artifacts.md) (a late sandbox final adds a second, separate risk revision; the round verdict and delivery stay unaffected). Binds the policy engine (5.2) and quarantine (5.3). Does not change [ADR-0025](0025-message-lifecycle-state-machine.md), [ADR-0034](0034-bec-ai-layer-invariant.md) or [ADR-0037](0037-dlp-policy-actions-and-lifecycle-binding.md).

**Context.** The score must be stored per round, idempotent by round, kept across rescans, readable by the scans API and available to whatever 5.2 builds. Two homes were possible: the policy-engine consuming `mail.message.scanned.v1` from the dormant `email.policy.evaluate` queue, or the email-worker at round finalization. The development pass-through (local/ci) consumes `scanned.v1` from the outbox concurrently with any queue consumer and must be able to use the score only to hold more. `scanned.v1` is strict and cannot gain fields without breaking consumers during a rolling upgrade (ADR-0034 amendment). The sandbox is asynchronous and its final may arrive before or after round completion (ADR-0040).

**Decision.**

1. **The email-worker computes the score inside the round's completion transaction** (`ScanRepository.complete`, also reached by the deadline sweeper), under the message and `email_scans` row locks, from the finals it already reads plus the round's `email_auth_results`, `bec_scan_results` and `dlp_scan_results` rows (echo-checked writer reads), and writes one `message_risk_scores` row (revision 0) plus `email_scans.verdict`, `risk_score`, `scoring_version`, before the lifecycle transition and the `scanned.v1` outbox row. Whatever consumes `scanned.v1` finds the row. A scoring error writes a fail-safe error row instead of blocking the round; a database error rolls back the whole completion as today.
2. **Next-round-only profile changes:** the round stores the tenant's active profile version id (`email_scans.risk_profile_version_id`, NULL = platform default) when it opens; completion uses that snapshot. Published versions are immutable, so the worker caches them by id.
3. **Sandbox as revision 1:** revision 0 never includes the sandbox. When both the round is complete and a non-skipped sandbox final exists, whichever transaction comes second (`complete` or the sandbox `writeRoundFinal`, both holding the `email_scans` lock) writes revision 1 with the same inputs plus the sandbox. Unique `(scan_id, revision, received_at)`; a rescan is a new round with new rows; rows are never updated.
4. **Reads:** the scans read API (replica, tenant-scoped, `message:read`) lists each round's revisions; a dry-run endpoint scores a stored round against a draft, published, platform or active profile from the replica and writes nothing.
5. **No new event, no topology change, no new deployable.** `apps/policy-engine` stays a skeleton until 5.2.
6. **5.1 enforces nothing new outside local/ci.** The development pass-through keeps every 4.x hold and additionally holds a round whose revision-0 recommendation is `require_approval`, `quarantine` or `block`, that has a hard block or an error row, or whose row is missing (switch `DELIVERY_DEV_PASSTHROUGH_RISK_HOLD`, default `true`); it never reads revision 1.
7. **Binding for 5.2:** policy reads revision 0 for the event's `scanId` (missing = hold) and may read revision 1 if present; its action must be at least as strict as every hard block, the failure floor and the DLP binding; it may be stricter than the recommendation; it may relax a level-only recommendation only through an explicit, audited tenant rule; an AI-only raise never justifies a hold. Policy owns `email_messages.final_verdict`, `final_action`, `risk_score` and transitions E8–E13. **Binding for 5.3:** quarantine entries carry the recommendation and explanation; a release re-validates against the latest round's score, and releasing a hard-blocked message requires the approval path.

**Alternatives considered.** Policy-engine consumer on `email.policy.evaluate` (races the dev pass-through, needs a deployable, broker user and ledger before 5.2 exists, and scores a second transaction's view of the round); a `mail.message.risk_scored.v1` event (no consumer; topology change); including the sandbox in revision 0 when it happens to be first (order-dependent); resolving the profile at completion (a change during a 30-minute round would apply to it); updating one score row in place on a sandbox result (loses the reproducible record).

**Consequences.** Completion does three more indexed reads and one insert. Every completed round from 5.1 on has a revision-0 row (a missing row is an alertable defect). Policy 5.2 has a stable, versioned input with a documented strictness contract; locally, more mail may be held by the pass-through than in 4.6, which is the intended fail-safe direction.
