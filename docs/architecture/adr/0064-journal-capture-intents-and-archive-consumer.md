# ADR-0064: Journal capture through database-driven capture intents; the `email.archive` consumer with reconciliation; scan rounds linked by frozen snapshots; topology v12; lifecycle edge E25 retired

**Status:** Accepted (2026-11-17, after the step 8.1 local verification: the archive stack suite through Postfix and the containers — journal capture in three directions, categories, the `email.archive` consumer, the policy reconciler after a purged queue, duplicates, a worker killed mid-capture — the 3-node RabbitMQ cluster suite on topology v12 and the full regression gate; record [docs/plan/steps/23-journaling-archiving.md](../../plan/steps/23-journaling-archiving.md) §13, §14). Proposed by the step 8.1 architect on 2026-11-16 (design A2–A7, A25, A26). **Supersedes [ADR-0025](0025-message-lifecycle-state-machine.md) decision 6 for the `archived` state** (edge E25 is retired; the state stays reserved). Amends [ADR-0024](0024-rabbitmq-topology-retry-dead-letter.md) (topology v12) and [ADR-0045](0045-policy-action-semantics.md) (ARCHIVE stays `requested` in the evaluation and is now performed by the archive-worker). Implementation notes: the sweeps apply the safety lag as a stop condition, not a predicate (WP3 D1); the lease is written into `lease_until` and `next_check_at` (WP3 §2); a policy source re-opens a journal-skipped intent (WP3 D20).

Amended by [ADR-0088](0088-archive-cross-source-dedupe.md) (step 8.6): one archive record per message **or merged copy group** — when at least one copy comes from journal intake, copies with the same Message-ID and header fingerprint within the dedupe window share one record (`archive_record_sources`, `archive_dedupe_keys`; intents record `merged` and `archive_received_at`); gateway↔gateway copies keep one record per message.

**Context.** Step 8.1 must journal inbound, outbound and internal mail per organization, and rejected, quarantined and failed mail when the tenant asks for it, independently of the delivery decision, without losing records when scanning or delivery fails, without duplicates on retries, without reinjecting mail, and with a journaling intent that is recoverable before the gateway acknowledges SMTP acceptance. It must also consume the policy ARCHIVE action's `mail.message.archive_requested.v1` requests (step 5.2), which have waited in the dormant, lossy `email.archive` queue (drop-head at 10 000), and reconcile from `policy_execution_logs`. The SMTP ingest already commits the message row and the `received.v1` outbox row before answering `250` (`451` on any failure, ADR-0019). Core message data (`email_messages`, scans, auth results, risk, policy evaluations) is dropped after 6 months, and the AWS mail bucket expires processed mail after 30 days, while an archive keeps mail for years. ADR-0025 planned an edge E25 `delivered | rejected → archived` for this step.

**Decision.**

1. **Capture intents.** One `archive_capture_intents` row per message in archive scope (unique `(message_id, received_at)`), carrying its sources (journal, policy, rule reference), the settings generation, a status (`pending`, `captured_open`, `closed`, `skipped`, `lost`, `failed`) and `next_check_at`. Intents are the work queue of a capture scheduler that claims due rows with `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` and leases.
2. **Three sources.** (a) A leader-elected **completeness sweep** reads `email_messages` forward by id (registry ids are time-ordered) behind a safety lag longer than the ingest transaction timeout, so every message row is evaluated exactly once for journal eligibility; (b) the **`email.archive` consumer** (`eg-archive`, ledger `archive-worker.archive`) upserts policy-sourced intents; (c) a leader-elected **policy reconciler** reads `policy_execution_logs` forward by id and upserts an intent for every evaluation whose actions hold ARCHIVE `requested`. The committed message and outbox rows are the recoverable journaling intent; the SMTP path is unchanged.
3. **Capture points and categories.** A pure decision function maps the message's writer state to `wait`, `capture(category)`, `skip(reason)` or `lost`: the first delivery (or `delivered`) → `delivered`; `quarantined`/`held_for_review` → `quarantined` when included or policy-sourced (else wait for a release); policy or quarantine rejection → `rejected` when included or policy-sourced (else skip); `rejected (delivery_failed)` or `expired` → `failed` when included or policy-sourced (else skip). Policy-sourced intents ignore directions and scope but need entitlement and the tenant's switch. A missing message row or original object is `lost` (alerted). SMTP-time refusals have no message row and are never journaled.
4. **What a capture writes.** The ORIGINAL object (`eml_object_key`, never a rewritten or policy copy) is streamed, its SHA-256 re-verified against `email_messages.eml_sha256`, encrypted and stored with a journal report object and one frozen scan snapshot per linked round and risk revision; the record row, the intent update and an index-queue row commit in one writer transaction. Snapshots freeze the rows of the scan read model (moved to `packages/database`) and are mapped back by the api's live mapper. **Nothing is rescanned**; later rounds within an outcome window are appended, never replacing earlier ones.
5. **Idempotency and recovery.** Unique keys on intents and records, attempt-scoped write-once object keys, leases, `processOnce` on the consumer; failures before the commit leave unreferenced objects that reconciliation removes. Capture never sends mail.
6. **Topology v12.** `email.archive` becomes a **lossy work queue** (`work-lossy`) consumed by the archive-worker as `eg-archive`; its bindings are unchanged (only `archive_requested.v1` and its retry key); it is not in `RECONCILE_QUEUES` because the policy reconciler is authoritative. A lossy queue keeps the outbox relay from ever stalling on the archive. One role user is added (11).
7. **E25 retired.** The archive-worker never changes `email_messages.status`. Archive membership is the record, not a lifecycle state; `archived` stays in the enum as a reserved state without a producer.

**Alternatives considered.** Binding `received.v1` and `state_changed.v1` to the archive queue (about eight events per message: as a work queue an archive-worker outage stalls the relay at 1 000 000 messages, as a lossy queue drops flood the shared dead-letter queue). Writing the intent in the smtp-adapter's ingest transaction (touches the hot path; the committed rows are already recoverable). Producing E25 (erases the delivered/rejected outcome from every status-driven surface and cannot represent archived quarantined mail). Capturing at receipt and discarding later (S3 writes and deletes for every excluded spam message). A new queue policy without dead-lettering (unneeded at the volume of ARCHIVE requests only).

**Consequences.** Journal capture latency is bounded by the sweep interval and the first re-check (about one to two minutes) instead of event latency; completeness does not depend on any queue. Archive-worker outages delay capture but never the mail path; capture must happen within the mail-bucket retention (30 days on AWS), so pending-age alerts fire at 6 and 24 hours. Rescans after the outcome window are not linked. The lifecycle documentation and the reachability invariant treat `archived` as reserved.

**Amendment (closing round of step 8.1, 2026-11-17).** A message waiting in quarantine (decision row 4 without `include.quarantined`) is no longer subject to `ARCHIVE_INTENT_MAX_AGE_DAYS`: the wait is bounded by `QUARANTINE_WAIT_MAX_DAYS` (100 days, above the longest quarantine hold plus review), so mail held longer than 25 days and then released is still journaled. The wait is marked on the intent (`category = 'quarantined'`), is not a capture backlog (`archive_intent_oldest_pending_seconds` counts only overdue or erroring intents) and not a capture issue; `archive_capture_intents` keeps 4 months (was 2) so a waiting intent is never dropped with its partition. Intents that still age out are counted (`archive_capture_skipped_total{reason="max_age"}`, alert `ArchiveCaptureAgedOut`) and listed as capture issues (`status=skipped`, code `max_age`).
