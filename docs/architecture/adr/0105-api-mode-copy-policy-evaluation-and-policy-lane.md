# ADR-0105: Policy evaluation of API-mode copies (detect mode) and the policy lane of remediation

**Status:** Accepted (2026-11-27, local verification: stack suite `api-mode-parity.test.ts` 11/11 — a tenant REJECT of a block-listed sender on a journal copy stored as a `policy_execution_logs` detect row (`evaluation_mode = detect`, `delivered_object = none`) and moved to the hidden quarantine folder by the policy lane (`api-remediation-decision/2`, winner `policy`), with no delivery row, DSN, `email.received` webhook or `mail.policy.decided.v1`; an internal DLP quarantine remediated through the policy lane; an outbound card-number message recorded as an `outbound` copy on the sender mailbox, `skipped outbound_not_remediable`, no simulator call; a hybrid inline-authority copy left `policy_state = inline` without evaluation; a `mailboxes` scope excluding one mailbox (`skipped out_of_scope`); policy-runtime, mailbox-api, policy-engine and mailbox-worker suites of WP2/WP3; live provider tenants, CI and Kubernetes apply pending); proposed by the step API-4 architect, 2026-11-27. Design: [docs/plan/steps/38-api-mode-parity-onboarding.md](../../plan/steps/38-api-mode-parity-onboarding.md) §0, §1.2 (items 1–4, 7), §2 (P-I1–P-I5), §3, A1–A6, §7, §12. Contracts [§39](../contracts.md#39-api-mode-parity-and-onboarding-step-api-4) (documents `api-remediation-policy/1` + `policyMapping`, `api-remediation-decision/2`; `policy_execution_logs.evaluation_mode`; `policy_state` vocabulary, skip reasons `outbound_not_remediable` and `policy_allow_override`, post-delivery statuses and reasons, copy direction `outbound`); migration `20261127120100_api_mode_parity_onboarding` ([docs/database/README.md](../../database/README.md#step-api-4-migration-20261127120100_api_mode_parity_onboarding)). Related: [ADR-0043](0043-policy-dsl-and-deterministic-evaluator.md) (the evaluator), [ADR-0100](0100-api-mode-intake-mailbox-worker.md) (the copies role), [ADR-0102](0102-mailbox-located-quarantine-entries-release-as-restore.md) (mailbox entries; a policy `require_approval` creates a `review` entry), [ADR-0106](0106-api-mode-detections-alerts-webhooks.md) (detections in alerts and webhooks). **Supersedes in part** [ADR-0098](0098-protection-modes-and-detect-mode-copies.md) (decisions 5 and 7), [ADR-0044](0044-policy-evaluation-placement-and-delivery-gating.md), [ADR-0101](0101-api-mode-remediation-policy-and-mailbox-actions.md) (decision 1) and [ADR-0104](0104-api-mode-internal-mail-and-reported-phishing.md) (decision 1).

**Context.** PO-D2 (decided 2026-11-27): API-mode mail must get the same tenant policy protection as inline mail (DLP, BEC, custom rules, allow/block lists). ADR-0098 (5, 7) keeps copies away from policy evaluation; ADR-0044 places policy evaluation in the policy-engine consumer; ADR-0101 (1) reserved a policy input; ADR-0104 (1) never records outbound-only journal messages.

**Decision.**

1. **Supersedes ADR-0098 decisions 5 and 7 in part and ADR-0044 in part:** copies with copy verdict authority are evaluated by the 5.2 evaluator with the policy-engine's runtime code (extracted to `@email-gateway/policy-runtime`) in the mailbox-worker `copies` transaction, in detect mode: stored as `policy_execution_logs` rows with `evaluation_mode = detect`; no lifecycle edge, delivery, hold, gateway quarantine entry, policy copy, `mail.policy.decided.v1`, archive request or sender notice. Organization setting and platform kill switch; failures fall back to the verdict-only decision.
2. **Supersedes ADR-0101 decision 1 in part:** the remediation decision has a verdict lane (unchanged rules) and a policy lane (document-level `policyMapping`, defaults quarantine/require-approval/reject → quarantine folder, tagged allow → tag; never hard delete); the stronger action wins; a disposition that only restates the platform default's risk recommendation (decision source `platform_default` with no floor binding at that level and no deterministic BEC hold; for an allow, only the platform risk tag) feeds no policy lane, because the verdict lane already decides it (closing-round review fix, note 38 §15.8); a tenant allow override suppresses verdict-only remediation; decision record `api-remediation-decision/2`; a policy `require_approval` creates a `review` mailbox entry. Post-delivery-meaningless actions are recorded as not applicable with reasons.
3. **Supersedes ADR-0104 decision 1 in part:** outbound journal messages are evaluated; a detection is recorded on the sender's mailbox as an `outbound` copy that is never remediated, never a clawback target and never a coverage sighting.
4. Hybrid inline-authority copies are never evaluated or acted on.

**Consequences.** Inline-equivalent protection for API mode with one evaluator; a library extraction in the policy-engine; one migration; more evaluation rows; tenants can tune the mapping per disposition.

**Alternatives considered.** Evaluation in the policy-engine with a second event; a third fact reader; reusing `mail.policy.decided.v1`; one first-match rule list (design §7).
