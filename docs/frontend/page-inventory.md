# Admin portal page inventory (steps 7.1, 7.2, 7.3, 8.1, 8.4, 8.2, 8.5 and 8.6)

The 22 pages and 12 dashboard widgets of the admin portal as built and verified in step 7.1 ([design note](../plan/steps/20-admin-portal.md) §1.3/§1.4, [verification record](../plan/steps/20-admin-portal.md#14-verification-record)). Operation ids omit the `_v1` suffix. **Gate** is the `x-eg-permissions` of the primary operation(s): the page and its navigation entry are shown when the user may call **any** of them; the api stays authoritative (every write is checked again). **Real** means every row and number comes from the listed operations; **Unavailable** names the state the page shows instead of a missing feature and the step that delivers it.

Verified end to end on 2026-11-13 by `tests/portal-e2e` (Playwright, real api, MySQL writer/replica, Redis, MinIO; [running and testing](running-and-testing.md)): every page below rendered seeded data with no error state and no serious or critical axe violation as `organization_admin` (and `platform_admin` / `mailbox_user` where relevant), and the unavailable states are present.

Pages 9 and 10 were rebuilt in step 7.2 ([design and verification record](../plan/steps/21-email-explorer-details.md)) and verified end to end on 2026-11-14 by the same suite (`explorer.spec.ts`, `details.spec.ts` and the extended isolation, responsive, pages and pagination specs; 66 of 66 tests with `--retries=0` and in the normal run): every search field and filter against exact seeded results, saved views, the audited subject reveal, the 14 sections for five roles with the masked values of a read-only user, the preview requested only after the click, a quarantine release with approval and a rescan from the details, cross-tenant isolation, three viewports, axe (no serious or critical violation) on every new view.

Pages 13 and 14 were rebuilt in step 7.3 ([design and verification record](../plan/steps/22-policy-builder.md)) and verified end to end on 2026-11-16 by `policy-builder.spec.ts`, `policy-management.spec.ts`, `dlp-bec-config.spec.ts` and the extended pages and responsive specs (85 of 85 tests of the whole suite, no retry needed): a policy built with nested AND/OR groups, a NOT, three actions and an exception; server violations on the node; Test and Dry run equal to direct api calls; publish, new version, compare, roll back with the audit rows; role and tenant isolation; risk-profile drafts; settings; DLP and BEC editing; three viewports.

Pages 23–26 (Compliance) were added and the Settings archive tab and the Integrations archive card replaced their "arrives in 8.1" states in step 8.1 ([design note](../plan/steps/23-journaling-archiving.md) A28, §9.5; WP5 record in §13.5). They are verified by the component tests (masking by default, reveal values kept in component state only, role-gated rendering, the immutability confirmation, CAS dialogs, re-authenticated downloads, review tagging, completeness banners, `429` countdowns) and end to end by `archive.spec.ts` and `ediscovery.spec.ts` against the real api, the rebuilt archive-worker, MinIO and OpenSearch (the run and its counts are in §13.5 and §14 of the design note and in [running and testing](running-and-testing.md)). Compliance auditors can run a case to a downloaded export but cannot place or release legal holds (`archive:legal_hold` stays with organization admins, product owner decision 1). The details route `/archive/[messageId]` is guarded by `archive:read` (navigation `childGate`), the search by `archive:search`.

Pages 27–31 (Account security and Incidents) were added in step 8.4 ([design note](../plan/steps/24-account-takeover-core.md) §6, D18; WP6 record in §13): the ATO dashboard, the account risk explorer and account details, responses and approvals, ATO settings, and the generic, category-aware incident list, details (with the account takeover panels) and print report; the shell's attention badge on the ATO dashboard item. They are verified by the component tests (MSW, axe) and end to end by `ato.spec.ts`, `incidents.spec.ts`, the ATO test of `policy-builder.spec.ts` and the ATO responsive test against the real api and ato-worker with ATO rows written through the libraries' stores (§14 of the design note). The incident routes below `/incidents` are guarded by `IncidentsController_get` (navigation `childGate`), account details by `AtoAccountsController_get`.

Pages 32–34 (Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace connections) were added, the Integrations page's "arrives in 8.2" card was replaced by the connections section (row 20, gate widened with `integration:read`) and the Mail servers connectors tab shows `provider_relay` connectors read-only with a link to their connection (row 8) in step 8.2 ([design note](../plan/steps/25-m365-google-connectors.md) §6, D3, D13, D14; WP6 record in §13). They are verified by component tests (`src/features/integrations/providers/providers.test.tsx`: the server-computed wizard, preview dialogs with the change set, typed confirmation and `impact_changed`, credential forms without echo or browser storage, the consent callback's URL scrubbing, role gates, axe), the dev-fixture contract test of the 24 operations, and `provider-connections.spec.ts` against the real api, integration-worker and the Compose provider fake. Live verification against real Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace tenants is pending.

Pages 35–36 (Identity providers) were added and the ATO pages' "step 8.5" states were replaced in step 8.5 ([design note](../plan/steps/26-ato-provider-adapters.md) §7; WP6 record in §16/§17): the account's Mailbox changes tab (mailbox state by target domain and trait codes, provider signals, Refresh now, per-item Remove forwarding through 8.4's request dialog with `remove_forwarding_rule` and `params.itemId`), the identity providers summary of ATO settings, the dashboard's Identity providers card, provider statuses on responses as returned (`confirmed`, pending confirmation, unconfirmed after timeout, `unsupported` with its reason; never done without a confirmation), provider actions in the request dialog of mailbox accounts and the Integrations link card. Verified by the component tests (MSW, axe) and end to end by `ato-providers.spec.ts` against the api, the ato-worker, the integration-worker, the provider-fake and the idp-simulator; live provider tenants are pending (PQ7).

Step 8.6 ([design note](../plan/steps/27-journal-intake-and-archive-split.md) D13, D14; WP6 record in §13.6) splits the former archive area: **Settings → Journaling** (row 21: what is captured, and the journal intake sources, rows 38–39) and the **Archive** navigation section (renamed from Compliance: Search, Retention and lock with the moved Immutability and keys and Access settings tabs, Legal holds, eDiscovery and the new Access audit, row 37). Organization admins no longer see Search, Legal holds, eDiscovery or Access audit unless they hold an archive role (`compliance_officer`); assigning such a role asks for confirmation. Organizations on the archive-only plan (`serviceProfile = archive_only`, ADR-0090) do not see Dashboard, Reports, Email explorer, Quarantine, Mail servers, the Security section (with Policy builder) or the SMTP connectors card, their start page is Archive search (or Settings → Journaling without archive access), a hidden route shows "Not available for this plan", and the domain DNS records mark MX, SPF and DKIM "Not required for archive-only".
