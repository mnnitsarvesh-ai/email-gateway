# Step 8.6 brief: journal-only intake and the Journaling / Archive split

Approved by the product owner on 2026-11-17. Build after step 8.5.

## Why

Step 8.1 archives only mail that passes through the gateway. Some customers want **only** archiving and journaling, without pointing their MX at the gateway or routing their mail through it. 8.1 also presents journaling (capture) and archiving (storage and use) as one settings area, which makes it harder for customers and management to understand.

## Scope

### A. Journal-only intake (a new capture source for the existing 8.1 archive)

1. Per organization, a dedicated journal intake address (for example `journal-<org public id>@<intake domain>`) that receives copies of mail from the customer's own mail system. The customer's MX and mail flow stay unchanged.
2. Supported senders:
   - Microsoft 365 / Exchange Online journal rules (Purview), sending journal reports. Unwrap the journal report to recover the original message and the full envelope (every recipient including BCC, and the sender).
   - Google Workspace routing with "Also deliver to" (a BCC copy).
   - On-premises Exchange journaling and generic SMTP BCC (`always_bcc`).
3. Sender authentication: accept only mail proven to come from that organization's own provider tenant or configured on-premises servers. Use the step 8.2 provider-connection binding (tenant identifiers, DKIM alignment, provider egress ranges) and per-organization intake secrets where possible. Refuse everything else, with no open relay and no backscatter.
4. The domain must be added and verified with the existing TXT verification. **No MX change is required.**
5. Captured messages go through the same 8.1 pipeline: idempotent capture, per-tenant encryption, Object Lock, retention, legal hold, search, eDiscovery and audit. The source is recorded as `journal_intake`.
6. Scanning is optional per organization (off by default): intake copies are archived as received. If scanning is turned on, the scan results are linked the same way 8.1 does.
7. The intake path must never deliver, forward or bounce mail to recipients. It needs dedupe across intake and gateway capture (for example, when a customer uses both), a size limit, rate limits, monitoring, and alerts when intake goes silent.
8. Setup guides and a wizard for Microsoft 365, Google Workspace and on-premises, generated per organization, reusing the 8.2 wizard and guide framework.

### B. Journaling / Archive split (presentation and permissions, one engine underneath)

1. Admin portal:
   - **Settings → Journaling**: what is captured (directions, categories, scope, policy-rule archiving, journal intake sources and their health).
   - **Archive**, a separate menu: retention policies, tamper-proof lock, legal holds, search and message view, eDiscovery cases and exports, access audit.
2. Permissions: journaling settings (IT and mail admins) are separate from archive search, eDiscovery and legal hold (compliance and legal). An IT admin who manages capture does not automatically get read access to the archive. Review the 8.1 permissions and add or split only where needed, with migrations for existing role grants.
3. API: no breaking changes. Group the endpoints under the two areas in the OpenAPI tags and docs.
4. Docs and the management "Mail Flow" page explain the two layers: Journaling (capture) and Archive (storage and use).
5. No change to the storage, encryption or database model of 8.1 beyond what intake needs.

## Out of scope

- Importing historical mail (PST or mbox import, or backfill from provider APIs). That can be a later step.
- Full-text search of message bodies (8.1 searches metadata only).

## Verification

- The normal full process: unit tests, API e2e, stack integration (intake through real SMTP from simulated Microsoft and Google journal senders, spoofed-sender refusal, dedupe, encryption, retention and hold), the whole Playwright suite and the orchestrator's full gate.
- Live tests with real Microsoft and Google test tenants, when the product owner provides them (as for 8.2 and 8.5).

## Product-owner decisions (2026-11-17)

1. **Archive-only plan:** a tenant can be "archive only": journal intake, journaling and archive, with no MX change and no mail filtering. The portal hides gateway-only pages (quarantine, routing and so on) for these tenants. It is offered as its own plan.
2. **Archive access:** add a new `compliance_officer` role with archive search, message view, eDiscovery and legal hold. Organization admins manage journaling settings and can assign the role (audited), but do not read the archive by default. Existing grants are migrated so no tenant loses access silently; record the migration in the design.
3. **Duplicates:** when the same message arrives through the gateway and through journal intake, store it once (matched by Message-ID and content fingerprint), record both sources, and merge the journal report's full recipient list (including BCC).
4. **Old mail:** 8.6 captures only new mail from the start date. Importing historical mail (PST/mbox or provider export) is a separate later step.
