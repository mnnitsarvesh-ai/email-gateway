# @email-gateway/policy-dsl

The policy language and evaluator of step 5.2 ([design](../../docs/plan/steps/16-policy-engine.md) P1–P12, P18, P19, P23; [ADR-0043](../../docs/architecture/adr/0043-policy-dsl-and-deterministic-evaluator.md), [ADR-0045](../../docs/architecture/adr/0045-policy-action-semantics.md)), extended by step 5.3 ([design](../../docs/plan/steps/17-quarantine.md) Q4, Q13.6, Q22, Q23; [ADR-0046](../../docs/architecture/adr/0046-quarantine-store-and-per-recipient-decisions.md), [ADR-0049](../../docs/architecture/adr/0049-policy-copy-body-edits-banners-and-url-rewrite.md)): per-recipient evaluation, release delivery edits, the WARN body banner request and policy-rewritable URL skips (evaluator `policy-eval/2`). Tenant policies are JSON documents `policy-dsl/1` validated against a closed catalog; the evaluator decides the final disposition of a scanned, scan-failed or parse-failed message from the round's facts, the active policies and the platform default `pol-default/1`, under a mandatory strictness floor. The policy-engine evaluates every round with it; the api uses it for validation, testing, dry-runs and the read views. The tenant-facing reference is [docs/architecture/policy-dsl.md](../../docs/architecture/policy-dsl.md) (its field table and examples are unit-tested against this package).

## Invariants

- **No customer code.** Policies are data. No `eval`, `Function`, `vm`, template or SQL; regular expressions only through RE2 (`re2js`, linear time, ≤ 256 characters, subjects cut to 1 024); globs through a bounded two-pointer matcher. Unknown keys, fields, operators and actions are refused with every violation by JSON path.
- **Never weaker than the floor.** `src/floor.ts` computes the floor from the facts only (hard blocks, failures, unknown engines, scoring errors, the DLP binding, missing risk or DLP rows, scan and parse failures) and the evaluator applies it after the tenant rules, the override and the URL_REWRITE raise on every path. No rule, exception, override or setting reaches it.
- **Missing data never relaxes.** Kleene three-valued logic: unknown facts never make a rule match or an exception apply.
- **No trust in spoofable identity.** Exceptions and the `when` of ALLOW/override rules see the sender only through authenticated fields.
- **AI never decides alone.** Rules reading `bec.verdict`/`bec.ai_raised` cannot hold mail (schema, and ignored defensively by the evaluator, including a URL_REWRITE `ifNotRewritten = quarantine` fallback since `policy-eval/2`); platform holds use deterministic verdicts.
- **Every recipient at least the floor.** Per-recipient evaluation (5.3) restricts only the recipient list; the floor and the recommendation are message facts (property-tested on 10 000 seeded inputs).
- **Pure and deterministic.** No I/O, clock, randomness or logger; the evaluation time is a fact of the input. Same input and snapshot → byte-identical result and `inputSha256` (property-tested on 10 000 seeded inputs × random policies).
- **No message content in results.** Results, explanations and header values hold ids, codes, enums, integers, hashes and policy data only.

## Tests

`pnpm --filter @email-gateway/policy-dsl test` (no infrastructure): every field × operator accepted or refused with its code; value types, limits at and beyond their bounds, exact violation paths; identity, exception and AI restrictions; RE2 refusals and known ReDoS patterns in bounded time; every operator true/false/unknown; Kleene tables; quantifiers on empty, full and truncated lists; time windows across Asia/Kolkata, America/New_York, Europe/London and Australia/Lord_Howe DST gaps and overlaps; ordering, stop, strictest-of-matched, exceptions, merge and applicability of every action; the strictness contract table; overrides and D2; snapshots, settings fallback and the snapshot hash; the input builder on real 5.1 risk rows (`scoreRisk` + `riskResultToRowJson`), caps and malformed rows; the row/view round trip (incl. 5.2 rows); the property tests; per-recipient evaluation (scoped-rule detection, quantifiers, department/user exceptions, floor for every recipient, limits, merged actions, release edits, URL rewrite eligibility, banner field); the DSL reference (field table, every example and the split examples). `test:integration` has no suites (pure library).
